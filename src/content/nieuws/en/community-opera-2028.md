In 2028 a very special project is on the programme: a **community opera**. As the name suggests, we are not just making this opera for the people of Delft, but above all together with them.

## Music by Krashna Musika

The orchestra and choir of Krashna Musika, the student orchestra and choir of Delft, form the musical heart of the production. Krashna Musika's permanent conductors are also involved: Ruben de Grauw (choir) and Stijn Berkouwer (orchestra).

Krashna Musika turns sixty in 2028, which makes the Community Opera the association's anniversary (lustrum) project as well.
