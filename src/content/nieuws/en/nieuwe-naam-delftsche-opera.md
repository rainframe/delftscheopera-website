Since 1994, Stichting Delftsche Opera Compagnie has organised large-scale productions for Krashna Musika, the student orchestra and choir of Delft. From now on we do so under a new name: **Delftsche Opera**.

## The curtain rises

The new logo is inspired by the theatre curtain: the moment the curtain opens and the performance begins. You will find that curtain throughout this website, framing our photos.

## New website, new addresses

Our website has moved to [delftscheopera.nl](https://delftscheopera.nl) and you can reach us at [info@delftscheopera.nl](mailto:info@delftscheopera.nl). The old stichtingdoc.nl addresses will keep working for the time being.

You can also find us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/delftsche-opera) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/delftscheopera).

## On to 2028

Meanwhile, we are working on our next big project: in 2028 we are creating a community opera, together with the people of Delft. [Read more about the Community Opera](/en/).
