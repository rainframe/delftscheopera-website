In 2028 Krashna Musika, the student orchestra and choir of Delft, turns sixty. We will celebrate this anniversary with a very special project: a **community opera**. As the name suggests, we will create it together with the people of Delft.

## Directed by Annemiek van Elst

The production will be directed by Annemiek van Elst. She worked as an assistant director with Simon McBurney, Barrie Kosky and Damiano Michieletto, among others, and recently directed the family opera _Atman!_, a co-production of Dutch National Opera, Opera Zuid and the Nederlandse Reisopera.

## Musical direction

Krashna Musika's permanent conductors are also involved: Ruben de Grauw (choir) and Stijn Berkouwer (orchestra).
