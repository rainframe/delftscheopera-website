In collaboration with stage director Floris Visser, Bizet's world-famous opera *Carmen* is performed. Over five sold-out evenings, the audience is swept along by the orchestra, the choir and highly talented soloists. For an extensive review, read [the article by Place de l'Opera](https://www.operamagazine.nl/featured/19527/tu-delft-verrast-met-krachtige-carmen/) (in Dutch).
